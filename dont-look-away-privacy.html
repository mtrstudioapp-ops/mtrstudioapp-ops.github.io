Privacy Policy

Don't Look Away by MTR Studio. Last updated November 10, 2026.

Don't Look Away ("the App") is a horror game. It needs no account and does not ask for your name, email or contacts.

Data stored on your device

Your night progress, souls, upgrades and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

Motion sensors

If "Look around" is on, the App reads your phone's gyroscope and gravity sensor to turn the in-game view as you turn your phone. These readings are used only on the device while you play and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about ad interactions and app performance. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA, UK and some US states, the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice in Settings under "Ad privacy options". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Children

The App contains horror content and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact email shown on the App's Google Play page.
